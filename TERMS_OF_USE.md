## 2. Description of the App

Quiet Practice is a personal journaling and mindfulness application that provides:

- **Daily Prompts** — guided writing prompts for reflective journaling
- **Rituals** — multi-day guided journaling experiences
- **Atmospheres** — ambient audio soundscapes for focused writing
- **Mood Mosaic** — a visual record of your daily emotional states
- **Letters to Future Self** — scheduled personal letters delivered at a chosen date
- **Stillness** — a space to collect and revisit your reflective practice

Some features require a **Quiet Practice Premium** subscription or lifetime purchase.
